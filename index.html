About us

Placeholder story content — replace with your restaurant's real background.

Coars Chicken started as a single smoker parked behind a hardware store. The idea was simple: cook chicken the way you'd cook it for family, over real wood, with no shortcuts.

Every bird is brined overnight, smoked low and slow, then finished over open flame to get a crisp, charred skin. Our sauces and rubs are all made in-house, in small batches, every morning.

Today we're still doing it the same way — one smoker, one grill, and a lot of patience.
